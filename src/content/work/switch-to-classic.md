## Why it exists

Salesforce admins often need the Classic interface for tasks Lightning doesn't cover. Switching back and forth by hand is slow.

## What I built

A small Angular Chrome extension that switches the current Salesforce org between Lightning and Classic in one click. I built it during my internship and stayed its main developer until February 2025.

## What changed

It was my first piece of software with real users outside the team, and the first thing I had to maintain over years rather than weeks.
